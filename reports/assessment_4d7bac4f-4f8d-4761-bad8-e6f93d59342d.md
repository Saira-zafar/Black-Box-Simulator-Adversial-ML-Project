## 3. Defensive Remediation Guidance

### Authentication Boundary & Token Validation Remediation:
Enforce mandatory authentication middleware or dependency injection (e.g., FastAPI's `Security` or `Depends(oauth2_scheme)`). Ensure any request lacking a valid, unexpired JWT/Bearer token is immediately rejected with HTTP 401 Unauthorized and WWW-Authenticate header.

### Specific Action Items:
- **Step 1**: Apply authentication dependency to the router.

- **Step 3**: Validate cryptographic signature and expiration claims on all tokens.
